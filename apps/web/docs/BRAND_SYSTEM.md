# My AfriAnalyze Brand System

## Core Ethos
Our design language must communicate: MODERN, EDITORIAL, FINANCIAL, INTELLIGENT.
- **NO crypto aesthetics:** No neon glows, dark mode default with bright accents, or "web3" vibes.
- **NO sparkles:** Avoid "AI magic" tropes. The intelligence should feel grounded, rigorous, and mathematical.
- **African-Market Aware:** Subtle nods through color and geometric patterns, but strictly avoiding tired stereotypes (no safari motifs, no overt tribal patterns). The focus is on the modern African economy.

## Typography
Strict and purposeful typography to support high-density financial data and editorial reading.
- **Primary / Editorial (Headings & Body):** A crisp, modern serif or high-legibility sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Roboto, or a clean serif like Merriweather for editorial headers).
- **Data / Numbers:** Monospaced or tabular figures are MANDATORY for all financial data to ensure vertical alignment. (e.g., JetBrains Mono, Roboto Mono).

## Color Palette
Restrained, professional, and trustworthy.
- **Backgrounds:** Off-whites and soft grays (e.g., #FAFAFA, #F5F5F5) for a paper-like, editorial feel.
- **Text:** Deep charcoal or ink blue instead of pure black for reduced eye strain.
- **Accents:** 
  - Subdued, earthy tones (terracotta, deep green, indigo) to reference the landscape without stereotyping.
  - Semantic colors (Green for positive, Red for negative) must be accessible (e.g., colorblind-safe shades).
